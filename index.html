1. Origin

I chose this topic because Google Translate often turns an English noun into a Chinese verb. For example, What is his obsession with you? becomes「他咋對你這麼癡迷？」: the noun obsession reappears as the verb 癡迷. This small mismatch is a useful window on what translators prioritize when they move a line from English into Chinese.

我選擇這個題目，是因為 Google 翻譯經常把英文名詞化成中文動詞。例如 What is his obsession with you? 變成「他咋對你這麼癡迷？」：名詞 obsession 在譯文裏變成動詞「癡迷」。這個細微差異讓我們能觀察譯者在英譯中時如何取捨。

2. Background

English commonly packages an event or state as a noun, while Chinese can make the same information more directly predicative. Translation studies describes such shifts through transposition and grammatical metaphor. Subtitle pairs are especially useful because the source and target sit side by side and the language is compact.

英文常用名詞承載事件或狀態，而中文可以把相同資訊更直接地放在謂語位置。翻譯研究以「詞性轉換」和「語法隱喻」解釋這些變化。字幕句對特別適合研究，因為原文與譯文緊密並排，語言也傾向精簡。

3. Gap

A list of isolated examples shows that noun-to-verb shifts exist but not how often they occur, which verbs recur, or whether the pattern differs across social acts, states, and events. The supplied corpus also lacks documented upstream provenance and independent human checking, so the current system exposes those gaps instead of presenting the data as validated.

零散例子只能證明名詞轉動詞存在，卻不能顯示出現頻率、常見動詞，或交際行為、狀態與事件之間是否有差異。現有語料亦缺乏清楚的上游出處與獨立人工核對，因此本系統公開展示這些限制，而不把資料說成已驗證。

4. Questions

  1. Which English nominals recur most often, and which Chinese verbs do they map to?
  2. Do communicative acts cluster more tightly than event or state nouns?
  3. How much of the corpus follows a direct noun-to-verb translation rule rather than contextual recasting?
  1. 哪些英文名詞最常出現？它們連向哪些中文動詞？
  2. 交際行為的譯法是否比事件或狀態名詞更集中？
  3. 多少資料可直接以詞性轉換解釋，多少需要按語境重寫？

5. Method

I received 500 aligned entries with five original fields: ID, English, Chinese, English_Nominal, and Chinese_Verb. The site preserves all 500 rows and five fields. The original row ID becomes part of the source trail. The browser converts the CSV into the brief's required fields without altering the texts.

我收到 500 筆對齊資料，原始欄位為 ID、English、Chinese、English_Nominal 與 Chinese_Verb。網站保留全部 500 列和五個原始欄位，並把原始列號納入出處紀錄。瀏覽器只在載入時轉換成作業要求的欄位，不改動原文。

Reproducibility record: 500 records; 195 distinct English nominals; 7 exact duplicate sentence-pair groups involving 15 rows, of which 8 are removable copies; no sampling seed supplied; no upstream URL/version supplied; 0 records marked human_checked=yes. 可重現紀錄：500 筆資料、195 個不同英文名詞、7 組完全重複句對（共涉及 15 列，其中 8 列是可移除的重複副本）；原檔沒有提供抽樣種子、上游網址或版本；目前 0 筆標為 human_checked=yes。

The rule order is: state/experience, communicative act, direct transposition, then contextual recasting. This priority prevents one entry from being counted twice. The duplicate filter removes 8 repeated copies while keeping one representative of each pair, leaving 492 unique textual pairs and making the 500-entry issue visible.

規則依次為：狀態／經驗、交際行為、直接詞性轉換、語境重寫。這個優次避免同一筆被重複計入。重複篩選會移除 8 個重複副本，同時保留每組一個代表，剩下 492 組不同文本，顯示「500 筆」與去重後數量的差距。

Outstanding quality check: at least 50 rows must be compared with the original source and edited by the student. This cannot be certified from the local CSV alone because it contains neither the upstream location nor the source snapshot.

尚待完成的人工檢查：學生須至少拿 50 筆資料與原始來源逐條比對。由於本機 CSV 沒有上游位置或原始快照，單憑這個檔案不能證實那些核對已經完成。

The review queue below is selected reproducibly with seed 2026. Export it, compare each row with the upstream source, and replace human_checked with yes only after verification.

以下核對隊列以種子 2026 可重現地抽樣。請匯出後逐筆與原始來源比較，只有真正完成核對的資料才可把 human_checked 改為 yes。

6. Analysis

The four linked views answer different parts of the same question. The Nominal–verb network shows concentration and recurring pairings; the Category treemap compares internal composition; the Keyword-in-context viewer restores evidence to its surrounding sentence; and the Evidence table supports sortable, row-level checking.

四個互相連動的檢視回答同一問題的不同面向：名詞—動詞網絡顯示集中程度與重複連結；類別矩形圖比較類別內部構成；詞語共現把詞放回句子的上下文中；證據表則支援可排序的逐列核對。

The chart is calculated from the same filtered records as the explorer. Its total must equal the evidence table count. Clicking a bar carries that category into the interactive views.

圖表與探索頁使用相同的篩選資料，各類別總和必須等於證據表筆數。點擊長條會把該類別帶入互動圖表。

7. Conclusion

The prototype makes a qualitative translation observation inspectable: a visitor can move from a recurring English nominal, through a Chinese verb, into the exact subtitle pair and back into the category scheme. It also makes data defects visible, especially duplicates, missing provenance, and the absence of verified rows.

這個原型把質性的翻譯觀察變成可檢查的系統：訪客可以由常見英文名詞、連到中文動詞、再回到實際句對與分類準則。系統同時公開資料問題，尤其是重複句、缺乏出處，以及尚未核對的資料。

8. Reflection

The difficult part was not drawing a network; it was deciding what counted as the same kind of shift. Priority rules made the coding reproducible but also hid ambiguity at category boundaries. With more time I would obtain the upstream corpus, verify 50 rows against it, replace the mechanical human_check field with a dated review log, and test the codebook on a second annotator.

最困難的不是畫網絡圖，而是判斷哪些轉換真的屬於同一類。優次規則令編碼可重現，但也掩蓋類別交界處的模糊性。若有更多時間，我會取得上游語料、核對至少 50 筆、以帶日期的核對紀錄取代機械欄位，並請第二位標註者測試分類準則。

AI could build the data pipeline, interface, and first analytical draft quickly. It could not certify that a translation belonged to the original source, make the writer's final judgment, or replace human verification.

AI 可以快速建立資料流程、介面與初步分析，但不能證實譯文確實出自某個原始來源，不能代替作者作最終判斷，也不能取代人工核對。
